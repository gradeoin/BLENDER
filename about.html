SACRED HERITAGE // COMPUTATIONAL PRECISION

NEXRAY 3D Atelier is an advanced digital sculpting and additive engineering studio based in Bengaluru, India. We bridge classical Indian sculpture with sub-millimeter additive manufacturing.

THE STUDIO MANIFESTO

ENGINEERED FOR THE SACRED, SCALED FOR REALITY.

For thousands of years, Indian temple sculptors followed the sacred geometry of the Shilpa Shastras—creating divine forms with profound proportions, mudras, and grace. However, when modern creators attempt to 3D print these deities using raw 3D scans or AI generators, the models fail: paper-thin cloth pleats break, bow staves collapse in mid-air, hands appear mutilated, and slicers crash on non-manifold edges.

NEXRAY 3D exists to solve this disconnect. We treat every digital sculpture as both a sacred work of art and an engineering challenge. Every bow stave is structurally anchored, every mudra is anatomically verified, every wall thickness is reinforced for physical polymer strength, and every mesh is mathematically sealed into a 100% watertight manifold.
