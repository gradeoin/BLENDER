How We Work With Clients

A seamless, three-stage pipeline from your photo reference to flawless physical 3D print.

01

Reference & Scope

Send us your photographic reference, sketch, or concept. We establish dimensions, physical print scale, and key iconographic requirements.

02

Sculpt & Manifold Check

We sculpt the model and run automated topology verification to ensure 100% manifold watertightness, zero non-manifold edges, and optimal overhangs.

03

Production Dispatch

You receive your verified binary STL, calibrated to exact millimeters, complete with tailored slicer settings for both FDM and Resin printers.
