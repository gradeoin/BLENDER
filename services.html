DISCIPLINE 02 // TOPOLOGY & MESH REPAIR

Watertight Manifolding & Slicer Optimization

Have an AI-generated 3D model (Meshy, Tripo, CSM) or raw 3D scan with non-manifold edges, open boundary holes, or overlapping inverted normals? Our proprietary topology repair pipeline reconstructs your geometry into a 100% watertight solid manifold that slices with zero slicer errors on any printer.

◆
Zero Non-Manifold Edges Strict manifold guarantees: 0 boundary edges, 0 zero-volume shells, 0 self-intersecting shells.
◆
True Flat Base Grounding (Z=0) Every pedestal and foot surface is precision-cut to Z=0.00mm, preventing mid-air starts or bed detachment.
◆
Hollowing & Suction Relief Internal 2.2mm wall shelling with dual concealed 3.5mm drain holes for resin suction release and chemical wash efficiency.
