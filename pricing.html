FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about commissioning 3D sculpts and payment terms.

How does the 50/50 payment milestone work?
To lock your production queue slot, you pay a 50% advance via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm). Once the sculpt is finalized and you approve the turnaround renders and web 3D model, you pay the remaining 50% balance, and we instantly dispatch the master STL package.
What file formats will I receive?
You receive the full-resolution Master Binary STL (compatible with Chitubox, Lychee, Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer, and Cura), an interactive Web GLB model for online viewing, and our official Slicing Handbook.
What is your Zero Slicer Error Guarantee?
If any slicer flags a non-manifold edge, zero-volume wall, inverted normal, or orientation hole on our files, notify our engineering desk at support@nexray.in. We will diagnose and repair the file within 12 hours at zero charge.
Can you adjust the size to match my altar or display case?
Yes! Use our calculator above to set your required height (from 3 inches to 14+ inches). We will scale the geometry with millimeter precision and adjust fragile features (such as bow staves and fingers) to ensure structural integrity at your chosen scale.
