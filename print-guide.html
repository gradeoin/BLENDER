The Official Slicing & Print Guide

Master settings, orientation strategies, and support protocols for printing Nexray 3D assets on Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality, and Resin printers.

FDM (Filament) Slicing Guide • Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer

All Nexray 3D models feature a pre-leveled planar base at Z = 0.00 mm. They seat flush on smooth PEI or textured beds without requiring rafts or brim attachments.

Setting Recommended Value Why it Matters
Layer Height 0.12 mm – 0.16 mm Resolves micro-relief on facial features and jewelry without visible stair-stepping.
Support Type Tree / Organic Supports Branches easily reach the chin, fingertips, and bow arch without scarring vertical drapery.
Support Overhang Angle 30° – 35° Ensures only critical overhangs are supported, cutting print time and material waste.
Infill Pattern & Density 15% – 20% Gyroid Gyroid infill provides isotropic physical strength and prevents nozzle dragging.
Seam Position Back / Aligned Hides layer transition retractions behind the cape and shoulder cloth.
Print Bed Contact No Raft / 0 mm Brim The built-in lotus pedestal is mathematically flat for direct adhesion.
💡 Pro Tip for Bambu & OrcaSlicer: Enable "Variable Layer Height" and set the smoothing quality around the crown and chin. This automatically prints the pedestal at 0.20 mm for speed and the face at 0.08 mm for ultimate detail!

Resin (SLA / DLP) Slicing Guide • Chitubox, Lychee

Resin printers reproduce 100% of the 2.20M polygon micro-details. For best results, follow this orientation and support protocol:

Setting Recommended Value Why it Matters
Print Orientation 35° – 45° Tilt (Backward) Minimizes peel force suction on each layer and prevents distortion across the flat base.
Layer Exposure 0.05 mm (50 microns) Standard 8K / 12K high-definition resin layer height.
Support Density Light to Medium Use 0.3mm contact tip diameter on the front to allow peel-off without leaving pits.
Hollowing (Optional) 2.0 mm Wall + 2 Drain Holes Saves 60% resin on a 6-inch idol. Place drain holes on the underside of the base.
Post-Curing Protocol IPA Wash (4 min) + UV Cure (3 min) Prevents brittleness and preserves sharp edge fidelity.
💡 Pro Tip for Resin Printing: Always place the heaviest anchor supports on the bottom edge of the pedestal, and keep front-facing supports strictly "Light" (0.25mm–0.30mm tips) so they snap off cleanly with warm water.
