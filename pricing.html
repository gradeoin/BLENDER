Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commissioning 3D assets with Nexray.

What file formats do I receive?

You receive the high-resolution binary STL file packaged in a verified ZIP archive, along with recommended slicer settings. Upon request, we can also provide OBJ or 3MF formats.

Can I rescale the 6-inch file in my slicer?

Yes! Because our meshes are 100% solid and watertight, you can scale them up or down in Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, or Cura without creating holes or geometry artifacts.

How do payments work?

For custom commissions, we accept UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, and Bank Transfer. We typically take an advance to begin modeling, and deliver the production file upon completion.
